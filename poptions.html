QuickSummarizer
Hover-based AI news summarizer
How to use
1
Activate the extension
Press Ctrl + Shift + S to toggle ON. A notification will confirm it's active.
2
Hover over any news headline
Hold your cursor over a link for 1.5 seconds. A progress bar at the bottom of your screen will appear to confirm intent.
3
Read the AI summary
A popup shows bullet-point highlights, sentiment (Positive / Negative / Neutral), and the article category — instantly.
💡 Saves API tokens: The 2.5s hover delay filters out accidental passes. Only deliberate hovers trigger a Gemini API call. Repeated visits use cached results instantly.
